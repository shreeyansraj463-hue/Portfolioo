I am Shreeyans Raj, an engineering aspirant currently focused on JEE preparation while exploring the intersection of software, physics, design and creative technology.

Front-end development became more than simply making websites for me. I became interested in what happens when code starts behaving visually: particles responding to movement, interfaces reacting to people, geometry becoming interactive and ideas turning into experiences.

Outside technology, I sketch, write poetry, listen to a wide range of music and explore ideas simply because I want to understand them.

01

POETRY · FROM BLOOM TO DUSK

From Bloom to Dusk

I saw a flower blooming,

As the morning sky made itself look clean and grooming.

The day before, it had just budded,

As the morning red turned white when the light flooded.

Now it is fully grown;

Now it is noon, and the sun has brightly shone.

As time passes, the flower withers;

Now it is evening, and the light slowly dithers.
